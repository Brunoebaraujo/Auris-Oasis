# Auris Oasis Vision

## Core Concept

A Diablo-inspired online RPG where players explore, discover rare artifacts, participate in events and influence a living economy.

## Long-Term Vision

A persistent online world containing:

- Unique characters
- Rare artifacts
- Marketplace
- PvE and PvP events
- Races and battlegrounds
- Player-driven economy

## MVP Scope

The MVP is intentionally small.

Player can:

1. Create a character
2. Enter a small world
3. Move freely
4. Find a collectible artifact
5. Store it in inventory

No multiplayer.
No NFT.
No economy.
No marketplace.
No combat.

The purpose is validating movement, immersion and collection loops.
