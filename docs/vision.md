# Auris Oasis Vision

## Core Concept

A Diablo-inspired online RPG, seen from a fixed isometric camera, where players explore, discover rare artifacts, participate in events and influence a living economy.

The Oasis is a virtual world in the spirit of *Ready Player One*. A cosmopolitan village (medieval architecture mixed with futuristic pieces) is the hub where every player meets before exploring other worlds.

Audience: ages 12 to 80. Platforms: desktop and mobile browsers.

Product context in Portuguese: [`contexto.md`](contexto.md).

## Long-Term Vision

A persistent online world containing:

- Unique characters
- Rare artifacts
- Marketplace
- PvE and PvP events
- Races and battlegrounds
- Player-driven economy

## World Structure

**The village hub.** The player begins in the village: safe zone, meeting point, respawn point, and home of the service NPCs (potion seller, weapons merchant — possibly a robot —, tailor, the Fairy who gives missions, and Aurelius, the arena master who leads to PvP). A portal plaza in the north connects the hub to every area.

**Forest (portal).** The first area, with the starting missions.

**Cave (portal).** The first exploration area, Diablo-style: imps, orcs, goblins and demons. A safe antechamber, then dungeon floors: first hand-made, later procedurally assembled from room templates, ending in a boss chamber.

**More worlds (portals).** Unlocked over time.

## Quests

Quests are missions composed of objectives (go to an area, collect, deliver, defeat, survive), not loose tasks.

Mission types: individual, collective, competitive and intergroup. Only individual missions work before multiplayer. Participation is always explicit: the player accepts an offer, or is assigned or invited.

## MVP Scope

The MVP is intentionally small.

Player can:

1. Create a character (warrior, barbarian, mage or rogue)
2. Enter the village hub and travel to the Forest
3. Move freely with click-to-move
4. Complete exploration and collection missions in the forest
5. Find a collectible artifact and store it in inventory

Basic combat is added before the cave (Map 2) is opened, since dungeons require it.

No multiplayer.
No NFT.
No economy.
No marketplace.

The purpose is validating movement, immersion, collection and quest loops.
